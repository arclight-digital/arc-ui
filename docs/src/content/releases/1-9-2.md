### Fixed

- **Top bar content alignment** — `arc-top-bar` now uses the same centering strategy as `arc-container` (`max-width` + `margin-inline: auto` + `padding-inline`) so nav content aligns pixel-perfect with page content at every viewport width. Consumers no longer need `::part(topbar)` padding overrides.
