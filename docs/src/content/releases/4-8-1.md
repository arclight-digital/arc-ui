Two fixes: a zoomed lightbox now reaches every edge of the picture, and the top bar stops leaving a gap where nothing is showing.

### Fixes

- **`arc-lightbox` zoom reaches every edge, and the caption stays readable.** With a caption under the picture, the pan limits assumed the image was centred in the stage when it sat higher, so part of the top and bottom was out of reach. The limits now come from where the image actually is, and end at the caption. A zoomed picture passes under the caption instead of over it, and the caption takes a backing while it does.
- **`arc-top-bar` closes the gap an empty actions box left.** Below the nav-collapse breakpoint, an actions slot with nothing showing (empty, or all hidden by your own CSS) no longer takes the row's gap on both sides, and the menu button sits a small step from its neighbour, so a `mobile-center="end"` search and the menu read as one pair. If you hid `::part(actions)` or pulled an item toward the menu with a negative margin to get this, both can go.
