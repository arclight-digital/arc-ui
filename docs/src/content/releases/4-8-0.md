The lightbox and the code block get their second versions, a new `arc-code-group` puts variants of one command behind tabs, and every page of these docs got a plain-English edit. Fixes from getpulsar.dev are in too.

### Lightbox v2

- **Touch.** Swipe sideways to step; the picture follows your finger and snaps back on a short swipe. Swipe down to close.
- **Real zoom.** Pinch or Ctrl + wheel from 1x to 4x, anchored where you point. Double-click or double-tap zooms 2x on the spot. While zoomed, the arrow keys pan and `0` resets.
- **Loading and errors.** A quiet spinner after 150ms, and an error state with the alt text instead of a broken image. The images on either side are preloaded, so stepping is instant. Entries take `srcset`, `sizes`, `width` and `height`.
- **Controls that read over any photo.** The bar and arrows sit on a solid backing (`--lightbox-control-bg`) and fade after 2.5s idle. A tap or a mouse move brings them back. Home and End jump to the ends. Screen readers hear "3 of 12" and the alt text.
- **`thumbnails`** adds a filmstrip under the picture. It rests dimmed and wakes on hover; the current image is marked with an accent bar.
- **Motion.** The picture grows out of the thumbnail you clicked, slides in the direction you step, and shrinks back into the thumbnail of the image you close on. The close still goes through the cancelable `arc-close` first. Reduced motion gets none of it.
- **`gallery="#photos a"`** turns existing `<a href><img></a>` links into the gallery. No script, and links added later work too.
- **An `actions` slot** in the bar for your own buttons, like download or share.

### Code block v2

- **Shell colors.** A command, its subcommand, flags, paths and variables each get their own color, instead of everything after `sudo` in one green. The colors are `--shiki-token-*` properties you can override.
- **`prompt`** adds a `$` (or your own string) before each command. Continuation lines don't get one, and it is never selected or copied.
- **`label`** is a plain title in the body font. `filename` stays mono and follows it when both are set.
- **A slimmer header.** 36px, the language as a quiet lowercase tag, and an icon-only copy button that turns into a check.
- **Reading aids:** `line-numbers`, `highlight="2,4-6"`, diff tinting (automatic for `language="diff"`), `wrap`, and `max-lines` with a "Show all" button that fires `arc-toggle`.
- **No reflow when colors arrive.** The plain render and the highlighted one have the same line boxes, and the colors fade in.
- **`arc-copy-button`** gains `icon-only` and `label`.

### New: arc-code-group

Tabs over a set of `arc-code-block`s, named by each block's `label`. One copy button copies the visible block. `sync-key` keeps every group with the same key on the page on the same tab and remembers the choice.

### Fixes

- **`arc-toast` no longer widens a phone page.** Its container ran 24px past the right edge on narrow screens in every position.
- **`arc-top-bar` on phones.** In centered mode the center column now gives way before the brand or the actions shrink, so its content can't draw over them. The side padding steps down to 16px under 480px.
- **`arc-top-bar` `mobile-menu="none"`** renders no hamburger, for a site with no menu to open.
- **`arc-top-bar` `mobile-center="end|hidden"`** moves the center slot next to the actions on phones, or drops it.
- **`arc-sheet`**: the backdrop enters on the panel's curve, so the two arrive together. A snap-point drag now follows the pointer directly; the height used to ease behind it and the release read a height the panel hadn't reached.
- **Six components that never animated now do:** the drawer panel, rail, float bar, skip link, and the select and tree-select chevrons. Their transitions named two timing curves, which made the whole declaration invalid. A check now catches it.
- **Docs:** the masonry page no longer claims items go into the shortest column (CSS columns fill top to bottom, then balance). Several component descriptions lost duplicated or stray text in the edit.
